How it works

Three steps, no examiner scheduling

1

The applicant talks

They open the web or Android app and speak naturally. The AI examiner listens, understands and responds like a real person, not a rigid form.

2

The AI examines

It covers the complete MER, body measurements, 17 medical condition categories and a women's health section, with intelligent follow up questions whenever a condition is flagged.

3

Your report arrives

A signed MER PDF in your branding, including the ID photo page, lands in your system automatically and is saved in the applicant's Reports section.

Capabilities

Built for real applicants, not ideal ones

People do not answer medical questionnaires in neat yes and no replies. The AI examiner is built for how India actually speaks.

A conversation, not a form

Real time voice AI holds a natural dialogue. It interprets rambling, hedged or indirect answers correctly, so "just occasionally, maybe once a week" is captured accurately, with no keyword scripting. Disciplined turn taking means background noise never derails the exam.

Every language your applicants speak

The applicant can even switch language mid sentence and the conversation continues seamlessly. True pan India reach with no language barrier.

EnglishHindiPunjabiTamilBengaliKannadaTeluguMalayalamMarathiGujaratiOdiaUrduand more

Complete MER coverage

Body measurements, 17 medical condition categories and a women's health section. When a condition is flagged, the examiner asks what, since when, and whether the applicant is on medication.

A doctor is one ask away

If the applicant asks to speak to a doctor, the app hands off instantly to a live video call with a doctor that connects automatically.

Identity and integrity built in

Photo ID capture at the start, a digital signature at the end, and the full session recorded for audit. Every examination stands up to scrutiny.

The report writes itself

A signed MER PDF in your brand and format is generated automatically, pushed to your system, and saved in the applicant's app for their own records.
